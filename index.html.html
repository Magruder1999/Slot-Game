WILD

WILD substitutes for every symbol above (high and low) to complete winning combinations. It does not substitute for the Grenade or the Cache symbol. WILD appears noticeably more often during the Rambo Bonus.

GRENADE — RAMBO BONUS

Land 3, 4, or 5 Grenade symbols anywhere on the base grid and they detonate to trigger the Rambo Bonus: free spins with a 2x win multiplier played out on an expanded 5x5 grid. 3 grenades = 8 spins, 4 grenades = 12 spins, 5 grenades = 15 spins. Landing 3 or more Grenades within the 5x5 bonus grid detonates them again for +3 extra spins.

CACHE — SUPPLY DROP HOLD & SPIN

Land 6 or more Cache symbols anywhere on the grid — including inside the 5x5 Rambo Bonus grid — to trigger Hold & Spin. Every Cache is a glowing orb that always shows a live dollar figure right on the symbol. The feature always plays out on a 3x5 grid: a convoy rolls across the screen while Supply Drop mobilizes, then every Cache symbol locks in place with its exact awarded value (or a jackpot tier, shown by the orb's glow color) baked right into it. You get 3 respins to fill the remaining positions — any new Cache symbol resets the respins to 3. The feature ends when respins run out, or the grid fills completely, which pays the GRAND jackpot outright.

PROGRESSIVE JACKPOTS

MINI, MINOR, MAJOR, and GRAND grow with every spin you make — watch the live meter above the reels. They scale with your denomination, and landing that tier's Cache symbol during Hold & Spin pays out the full current jackpot, which then resets to its seed value and starts climbing again.

DENOMINATIONS

Choose 10¢, $1, $5, or $10 denomination. Each has its own 5 selectable bet levels, and jackpots and win sizes scale to match — the 10¢ denomination plays small and slow, $10 plays big and fast.
